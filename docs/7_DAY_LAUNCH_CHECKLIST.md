# 7-Day Founder Launch Checklist: First Customer Outreach

Follow this day-by-day checklist to get your first meetings and evaluation agreements with chipmakers and investors.

---

## Day 1: Polish the Public GitHub Profile (1 Hour)
- [ ] Push all code, datasheets, and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` to your GitHub repo.
- [ ] Ensure the README has the clean badges (CI Passing, C++20, AXI4-Full).
- [ ] Pin this repository to the top of your personal GitHub profile.

---

## Day 2: Set Up Your Founder LinkedIn & Email (1 Hour)
- [ ] Update your LinkedIn headline:  
  `Founder & Chief Architect @ HyperRAM | Building Hardware Memory Compression to Beat AI Inflation`
- [ ] Add a short summary:  
  `Building next-generation synthesizable AXI4 memory controller IP blocks that deliver 2:1 hardware lossless memory compression for consumer and Edge AI SoCs.`

---

## Day 3: Build Your Top 25 Prospect List (2 Hours)
Search on LinkedIn for **"SoC Architect"**, **"VP of ASIC Design"**, or **"Hardware Engineering Director"** at:
1. **Rockchip** (Budget tablet/laptop SoCs)
2. **Allwinner Technology** (Budget consumer SoCs)
3. **SiFive / StarFive / Milk-V** (RISC-V SoCs)
4. **Espressif Systems** (Low-power IoT chips)
5. **Hailo / Axelera AI** (Edge AI NPUs)
- [ ] Collect 25 names and their LinkedIn URLs in a simple sheet.

---

## Day 4: Send the First 10 Cold Outreach Messages (1 Hour)
Use the message template from `docs/CUSTOMER_ROI_AND_PITCH.md`:
- [ ] Send 10 personalized InMails / Connection Requests.
- [ ] Keep the message under 100 words. Focus strictly on the **$15–$25 BOM cost reduction**.

---

## Day 5: Send the Remaining 15 Outreach Messages & Follow-ups (1 Hour)
- [ ] Send the next 15 messages.
- [ ] Reply to any responses asking for the datasheet by attaching `docs/ip_package/HYPERRAM_IP_DATASHEET.md`.

---

## Day 6: Practice Your 3-Minute Pitch (30 Mins)
- [ ] Open `docs/INVESTOR_CHEAT_SHEET.md`.
- [ ] Read the 30-second hook aloud 5 times until it feels natural.
- [ ] Practice running `.\build\Release\hyper_ram_sim.exe` in your terminal.

---

## Day 7: Book the First 10-Minute Technical Evaluation Call
- [ ] When a lead responds positively, send a Calendly / Google Meet link.
- [ ] Goal for Call 1: Deliver the 30-second hook, show the 10-second terminal demo, and offer to send them our 30-day evaluation package under NDA.
